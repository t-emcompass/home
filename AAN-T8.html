AAN T8

Asymmetric Artificial Network — Unshielded Twisted Pairs · RJ45

About this product

The AAN T8 is a purpose-built Asymmetric Artificial Network (AAN) — standardised as an Impedance Stabilisation Network (ISN) — for conducted emission measurements on unshielded twisted pair (UTP) cables with an RJ45 interface.

It establishes a defined, reproducible common-mode impedance of 150 Ω at the cable port of the Equipment under Test (EuT), enabling accurate and repeatable conducted emission measurements.

The T8 variant supports up to four unshielded pairs simultaneously, making it suitable for both Fast Ethernet (100BASE-TX) and Gigabit Ethernet (1000BASE-T) applications — covering the vast majority of modern UTP-based equipment in a single compact unit.

Key features

⚡

Stable 150 Ω Common-Mode Impedance

Defined, reproducible impedance across the full 150 kHz – 30 MHz range for consistent, comparable results.

🔌

1 to 4 Pairs — Single Unit

Covers 100BASE-TX (2 pairs) and 1000BASE-T (4 pairs) Ethernet in one device — no swapping between ISNs.

📏

50 Ω N-Type Measurement Port

A strong N-type connection to connect any spectrum analyser or EMI receiver.

🔒

PoE Compatible

Rated up to 60 V DC / 0,5 A per pair, fully compatible with Power over Ethernet operating conditions.
