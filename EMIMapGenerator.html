EMI Map Generator

EMI Heatmap Software · Near-Field Scanning · Multi-Analyser

About this product

The EMI Map Generator is a practical software tool that turns your existing near-field probe and spectrum analyser into a fully functional EMI heatmap scanner. Using a standard USB webcam as a position reference, it overlays measured electromagnetic emission data directly onto an image of your PCB or device under test.

The result is a clear, colour-coded heatmap that immediately reveals the dominant emission sources on your board — no dedicated XY scanner or specialist hardware required. Designed to be accessible for any engineer who already has a spectrum analyser and a near-field probe set.

The software supports the Rohde & Schwarz FPL series, Siglent Technologies SSA3000X series, and RIGOL Technologies DSA series spectrum analysers, and works with any standard near-field probe.
