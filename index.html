What we do

We develop and deliver tools that help engineers efficiently troubleshoot electronic systems, with a sharp focus on EMC. Our mission is entirely centred on debugging tools — not product design, not consultancy.

Hardware Tools

Specialised hardware to simplify and speed up troubleshooting:

  • Impedance Stabilisation Networks (ISNs)
  • Measurement probes
  • Immunity testers

Software Solutions

Software that supports and accelerates troubleshooting — from VISA instrument control scripts to 2D EMI near-field heatmap generation.

Support & Education

  • Expert guidance on selecting the right tools
